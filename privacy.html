Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026  •  Application: Crib Ready Publisher  •  Contact: luigisjob@gmail.com

1. Overview

Crib Ready Publisher (@CribReady) is an original content-production and publishing application operated by Luigi Amari (Palermo, Italy) solely to create, manage, and publish educational pregnancy, maternity, and newborn videos and printable checklists to Crib Ready’s official channels, including Pinterest Business.

2. Information Accessed via API

When authorized via official OAuth 2.0 consent (such as Pinterest API v5 scopes boards:read, pins:read, pins:write, and user_accounts:read), the app accesses only our authorized account profile, boards, and Pin publishing endpoints in order to display destination boards and publish original Video Pins linking to Crib Ready resources.

3. Storage, Security & No Third-Party Sharing

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator’s workstation strictly for authenticated API calls on behalf of @CribReady.
  • Crib Ready Publisher does not collect, harvest, store, sell, rent, or share personal information of third-party users or external Pinterest accounts.
  • No third-party tracking cookies or external advertising SDKs are used inside the publishing studio.

4. Revocation & Deletion Requests

Authorized API access can be revoked at any time from your platform security settings (e.g. Pinterest Security & Logins) or via the Disconnect button in Crib Ready Studio, which deletes locally stored tokens immediately. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, email luigisjob@gmail.com.
